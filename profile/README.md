# Raethexn Technologies

**Building systems for a world where AI becomes part of how we think, work, and create.**

Raethexn Technologies is an independent Canadian AI systems studio exploring the infrastructure emerging around increasingly capable AI.

We design and build systems across **AI memory, agents, natural language interfaces, intelligent workflows, and cloud-native infrastructure** — turning ideas about the future of software into things that can actually be used, tested, and understood.

Our work is driven by a simple belief:

> **The most interesting questions about AI should be explored by building the systems that make them real.**

## What We're Exploring

AI is changing more than the applications people use. It is changing the assumptions underneath software itself.

What happens when conversation becomes an interface?

What happens when software can reason across tools, systems, and years of context?

What should an AI remember — and who should own that memory?

How do people maintain continuity when their digital lives are increasingly spread across models, agents, and platforms?

Raethexn exists to explore questions like these through **working software**.

## OpenMemory

### Your history with AI, in one place you control.

[**OpenMemory**](https://github.com/Raethexn-Technologies/OpenMemory) is our flagship open-source project exploring what comes after platform-bound AI memory.

Today, a person's history with AI is fragmented.

A career decision might live in ChatGPT.
A programming breakthrough might live in Claude.
A research thread might live in Gemini.
Months or years of context become scattered across systems that cannot understand the whole.

**The person is the constant. The provider should not be the boundary around their memory.**

OpenMemory brings those histories together into a provider-neutral, user-controlled corpus.

It combines two forms of memory:

**Historical memory** — conversation archives imported from AI providers while preserving their source, chronology, branches, and provenance.

**Live memory** — durable knowledge that agents and AI tools can write and recall across environments through a shared memory layer.

Together, they create something more interesting than another conversation search tool.

They create the possibility of asking questions across your accumulated history with AI:

> When did I first become interested in this?

> How has my thinking changed?

> What ideas do I repeatedly return to?

> What did I plan to do and never mention again?

> What patterns only become visible when years of conversations are viewed together?

OpenMemory is built around an important constraint: **those answers should be grounded in evidence.**

Observations trace back to the conversations, messages, providers, and timestamps that support them.

The goal is not to have AI define who someone is.

The goal is to give people better tools for understanding their own history.

## How We Build

Raethexn operates between **research, product engineering, and deployment**.

We build:

* applied AI and agentic systems
* memory and context infrastructure
* retrieval and knowledge systems
* natural-language interfaces
* intelligent workflows and automation
* cloud-native AI architecture
* experimental systems that investigate emerging computing models

Ideas are treated as engineering questions.

If an idea matters, we want to know what happens when it encounters identity, privacy, latency, security, unreliable models, real data, existing infrastructure, and actual users.

That is where the interesting work begins.

## Build in Public

Open source is part of how we investigate.

Projects are intended to be more than demos. They should be **inspectable technical artifacts**: architecture, experiments, failures, tradeoffs, research questions, and working implementations that other people can examine and extend.

## The Thesis

The next generation of software will not simply add AI features to existing applications.

Software itself is becoming more conversational, contextual, adaptive, and agentic.

That creates new infrastructure problems around **memory, identity, trust, coordination, ownership, and interoperability**.

Those are the problems Raethexn Technologies wants to work on.

**Build the systems. Test the ideas. Learn what comes next.**
